# Tax season assembled in advance

## When to Use
When I ask to assemble the tax pack, the accountant's list, or
what is missing for tax.

## Procedure
1. Read the checklist in ~/Business/Tax/checklist.md.
2. Search ~/Business/Invoices, ~/Business/Expenses and
   ~/Business/Finance/statements for matching documents.
3. Copy (never move) matches into a dated folder for this
   financial year, organised by the checklist's headings.
4. List what is still missing and where it might be.

## Pitfalls
- Copy, never move. The originals stay where they are.
- A document matching two categories goes in both; do not make
  an arbitrary choice.
- Bank statements for a joint or personal account are out of
  scope — business accounts only, listed in accounts.md.
- Never state or estimate an amount of tax owed. Assemble and
  flag only; the accountant decides.

## Verification
Every checklist heading must have either matched documents or an
entry on the gap list. Nothing silently absent.

## Stop and Ask Me Instead When
- My notes mention a change this year: a new entity, an asset
  sale, overseas income, a change of registration. Put it first
  on the gap list and tell me to speak to the accountant before
  anything else.
- I ask what is claimable or how much tax is owed. Say that is
  my accountant's question, and assemble the documents only.

## Approval Required
Nothing goes to my accountant until I have reviewed the pack.
Assemble and flag only; never estimate tax owed. Never send,
pay, post or delete anything without my explicit "approved".
