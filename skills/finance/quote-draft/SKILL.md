# Quotes and estimates, drafted in minutes

## When to Use
When I paste an enquiry and ask for a quote or estimate.

## Procedure
1. Read the enquiry I give you.
2. Find the three most similar past jobs in
   ~/Business/Quotes/sent/ and tell me what was charged and
   included, with dates.
3. Draft a quote using ~/Business/Quotes/rates.md and our
   standard terms.
4. Show your pricing reasoning before the quote itself.
5. List anything ambiguous that I should clarify before sending.

## Pitfalls
- Never reuse a past price without stating its date. Rates from
  two years ago are a trap.
- If the enquiry is missing quantities, dimensions or a
  deadline, ask. Do not assume a middle value.
- Rush jobs and out-of-hours work carry the surcharge in
  rates.md. Check the requested date before pricing.

## Verification
Every line in the draft must trace to a line in rates.md or be
explicitly marked as a judgement call.

## Stop and Ask Me Instead When
- The client is one I have marked strategic, the job is a formal
  tender, or I have said it is a loss-leader. Give me the
  comparable jobs and leave the price to me.
- The enquiry asks for non-standard terms, liability or a
  contractual commitment. List those terms for me; do not draft
  them.

## Approval Required
Send the draft quote to me only. Never send a quote to a
prospect. Never send, pay, post or delete anything without my
explicit "approved".
