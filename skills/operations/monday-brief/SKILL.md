# The Monday operating brief

## When to Use
Monday mornings, or when I ask where we stand.

## Procedure
1. Run: cash-forecast, invoice-chase (summary only), payables
   (totals only), follow-up-sweep (counts only), renewals (list
   only), obligations, mention-watch.
2. Take the headline from each. Do not reproduce full output.
3. Assemble one page in the order above.
4. Add up to three items that may need attention, each with its
   evidence and why it was surfaced. Decide nothing.
5. List anything that failed to compute, with the reason.

## Pitfalls
- One page is a hard limit. Cut detail, never sections.
- A skill that errors must be reported as failed, never silently
  omitted.
- Do not repeat last week's three items unchanged; if something
  persists, say how long it has persisted.

## Verification
Confirm all seven component skills ran, naming any that did not.

## Stop and Ask Me Instead When
- I ask you to recommend hiring, borrowing or letting someone
  go. Say the brief is not built for that, and point me to the
  full reports.
- Two or more component skills fail. Put that at the top and say
  the brief is incomplete.

## Approval Required
None; the brief is for me only. Never send, pay, post or delete
anything without my explicit "approved".
