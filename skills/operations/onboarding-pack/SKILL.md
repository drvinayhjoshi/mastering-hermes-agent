# The onboarding pack that isn't rebuilt each time

## When to Use
When I tell you someone new is starting and ask for their
onboarding pack.

## Procedure
1. Read ~/Business/People/onboarding-master.md and the role
   variations.
2. Build the new joiner's pack: day-one schedule, accounts to
   create, who to meet in week one, reading list.
3. Build my separate preparation list, dated backwards from the
   start date for anything with a lead time.

## Pitfalls
- Anything with a lead time (hardware, access requests,
  background checks) must appear dated backwards from the start
  date, not on it.
- Never include salary, personal details or anything from the
  recruitment file in the joiner's pack.
- Role variations override the master list; they do not
  supplement it. Check for contradictions and ask.

## Verification
List every master-checklist item and whether it was included,
varied, or excluded for this role.

## Stop and Ask Me Instead When
- The hire involves visas, background checks or anything legally
  sensitive. Add the task, dated, to my preparation list, but
  write nothing about the substance.
- The role will have access to money or sensitive systems. List
  that access for me to set up and approve; never put
  credentials or set-up steps in the joiner's pack.

## Approval Required
I review the pack before it is shared. Never include personal or
recruitment data. Never send, pay, post or delete anything
without my explicit "approved".
