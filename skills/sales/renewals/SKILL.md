# Renewals you see coming

## When to Use
When I ask about renewals, contracts ending, or which accounts
need attention before renewal.

## Procedure
1. Read renewals.md. Select agreements ending within 60 days.
2. For each, check: usage or orders vs own history, complaints
   in the last 6 months, late payments this year, days since
   last contact.
3. Record each signal with its evidence and source.
4. Attention = at least one signal present. Nothing else.
5. Draft a check-in for each attention account, to me only.

## Pitfalls
- Never state or imply what the customer intends. Report the
  signal ("requests down 37%"), not a prediction ("likely to
  leave").
- An agreement with no end date in the table is reported as "end
  date missing", not skipped.
- Drafts must not mention the renewal or the complaint. The
  first conversation about either is mine.

## Verification
Agreements listed = agreements in renewals.md ending within 60
days. State the count and the total annual value.

## Stop and Ask Me Instead When
- The account is in a dispute or a legal discussion. Report it,
  but write no draft.
- The renewal involves new pricing or new terms. Assemble the
  history only; no draft.
- It is one of my five largest customers by annual value. Report
  it and suggest I call; no draft.

## Approval Required
Report and drafts to me only. Never message a customer. Never
send, pay, post or delete anything without my explicit
"approved".
