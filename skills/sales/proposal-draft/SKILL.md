# Proposal first drafts from your own past work

## When to Use
When I share a brief and ask for a proposal draft.

## Procedure
1. Read the brief and list what is being asked for.
2. Use ~/Business/Proposals/won/ as the model for structure and
   tone.
3. Pull methodology and team sections from
   ~/Business/Proposals/boilerplate/ and tailor them to the
   client's sector.
4. Leave the pricing section blank.
5. Mark every place you have guessed at something in the brief.

## Pitfalls
- Won proposals are the model; lost ones are not in scope. Never
  draw structure from ~/Business/Proposals/lost/.
- Do not invent case studies or client names. If a relevant
  example doesn't exist in the folder, leave a placeholder
  marked [CASE STUDY NEEDED].
- Client-specific figures in past proposals are confidential to
  that client. Never carry a number across.

## Verification
List every section and where its content came from — brief,
boilerplate, past proposal, or newly written.

## Stop and Ask Me Instead When
- The brief asks for confidential pricing, complex contractual
  obligations or a non-standard legal commitment. Leave those
  sections as marked placeholders for me.
- It is a formal tender with strict compliance rules. Produce a
  checklist of the requirements instead of a draft.

## Approval Required
I read and approve every section before anything is sent. Never
send a proposal. Never send, pay, post or delete anything
without my explicit "approved".
