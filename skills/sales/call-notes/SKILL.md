# Call notes into the system, in ninety seconds

## When to Use
When I send notes or a voice note right after a call and say
"log it".

## Procedure
1. Match the company and person to the existing client list; ask
   if unsure rather than creating a new record.
2. Extract interest, objection, next action, date and
   stakeholders.
3. Write them to the CRM or the client's file.
4. Create the follow-up task for any deadline mentioned.
5. Confirm in one line.

## Pitfalls
- Voice notes mistranscribe company and people's names. Match
  against the existing client list and ask when unsure rather
  than creating a duplicate record.
- A stated deadline ("pricing by Friday") is a commitment.
  Always create the task, never just note the sentence.
- Do not summarise away the objection. The objection is the most
  valuable field; record it close to verbatim.

## Verification
Echo back the four key fields — interest, objection, next
action, date — so I can correct a mishearing immediately.

## Stop and Ask Me Instead When
- The notes cover health, legal, HR or personal matters. Keep
  those parts out of the CRM and tell me what you left out.
- A price or terms were agreed on the call. Record them as "to
  confirm in writing" and remind me to confirm them myself.

## Approval Required
Echo the four key fields back to me before saving anything to
the CRM. Never send, pay, post or delete anything without my
explicit "approved".
