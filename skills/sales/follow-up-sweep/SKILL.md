# The follow-up that doesn't get forgotten

## When to Use
When I ask who to follow up with, or for follow-up drafts on
quotes and proposals.

## Procedure
1. List everything quoted or proposed in the last 90 days. If
   you cannot find a pipeline file with last-contact dates, say
   so and draft nothing.
2. For each, find the last contact date and whether an outcome
   was recorded. Drop anything marked won or lost.
3. Count days from the last contact. A follow-up is due once 3,
   10 or 24 days have passed since the last contact and nothing
   has been sent since.
4. Leave out, and list for me by name with the reason, anyone who
   asked not to be contacted, went quiet after a complaint, or
   is at a delicate stage such as a final negotiation or a
   pricing objection. Draft nothing for them.
5. For each one still due, draft a short, warm follow-up, no more
   than five drafts per run, the one longest since its last
   contact first.
6. In each draft, use only facts you found in my files: the
   pipeline, my notes and the quote itself. Never write a
   result, a percentage, a past client, a feature or an offer
   that you did not find there. If a draft needs a real example,
   write [ADD A REAL EXAMPLE] in its place.
7. Before you show the drafts, check each one against the
   pipeline file: the quote date, the last-contact date and the
   amount in the draft must match it exactly.
8. Write the reply in two parts. Part 1, DRAFTS: the full text of
   every draft, each under the customer's name. Part 2, LEFT OUT:
   each customer you left out, with the reason. Never reply with
   only a list of names.

## Pitfalls
- A quote marked lost or won is closed. Never follow up on it.
- Do not send the same phrasing twice to the same person. Vary
  the angle; look in my sent-follow-ups file if there is one, or
  say there is none.
- If nothing new can honestly be added, say so and suggest a
  call instead of drafting an empty nudge.
- An invented result in a follow-up is worse than no follow-up.

## Verification
Count of drafts must equal count of opportunities past the
cadence threshold and not closed, or five, whichever is
smaller. Every date and
amount in a draft matches the pipeline file. No draft contains a
figure that is not in my files.

## When You Find These
- The prospect has asked not to be contacted, or went quiet
  after a complaint. Leave them out and list them for me.
- My notes show a delicate stage. Draft nothing; list it for me.

## Approval Required
Send drafts to me only. Never send a follow-up to a client.
Never send, pay, post or delete anything without my explicit
"approved".
