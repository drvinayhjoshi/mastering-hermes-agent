# Spotting the complaint you're getting repeatedly

## When to Use
When I ask what customers keep complaining about, or for the
monthly complaint patterns.

## Procedure
1. Read last month's support messages.
2. Group them by underlying problem, not wording.
3. List any problem raised by three or more different customers,
   with counts and a representative quote from each.
4. Order by frequency and say which fix would remove the most
   messages.

## Pitfalls
- Count distinct customers, never messages. One frustrated
  customer emailing five times is one data point.
- Group by cause, not by keyword. "Late", "still waiting" and
  "no update yet" are one problem.
- Do not propose solutions. Report the pattern; the fix is a
  business decision, not a summarisation task.

## Verification
Distinct customer count and total message count stated for each
cluster.

## Stop and Ask Me Instead When
- A single complaint is serious on its own: a safety issue, a
  data breach, an allegation against a person. Alert me straight
  away, outside the monthly report.
- Complaints name a member of staff. Report the count and theme
  without names, and tell me there are named complaints to look
  at.

## Approval Required
None; the report comes to me. Never send, pay, post or delete
anything without my explicit "approved".
