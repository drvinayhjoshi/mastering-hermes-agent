# The follow-up after it's fixed

## When to Use
When I ask for follow-ups on resolved issues.

## Procedure
1. List issues marked resolved four days ago.
2. Read each whole thread. Look for an explicit statement of
   satisfaction, or a "resolved, customer satisfied" status.
3. Draft a short check-in for each; add a light review request
   only where that explicit satisfaction exists.
4. Skip anyone on ~/Business/Support/no-ask.md.
5. Send the drafts to me and log who was asked.

## Pitfalls
- Only ask where the customer explicitly said they were
  satisfied, or the record says so. Never infer it from tone.
- Never ask for a review where the customer expressed
  frustration at any point in the thread, even if it ended well.
  Check the whole thread, not the last message.
- Never ask the same customer twice in twelve months.
- If the fix involved a refund or credit, check in but do not
  ask for a review at all.

## Verification
State how many were skipped and why, by category.

## Stop and Ask Me Instead When
- The issue involved a refund, a credit, an injury or a formal
  complaint. Draft a personal check-in only, with no review
  request.
- My notes say our sector or review platform restricts review
  requests. Draft check-ins without any review request.

## Approval Required
Send drafts to me only. Never message a customer. Never send,
pay, post or delete anything without my explicit "approved".
