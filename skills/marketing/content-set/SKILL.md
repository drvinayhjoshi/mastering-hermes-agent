# One idea, five channels

## When to Use
When I give you an idea and ask for the content set, or versions
for each channel.

## Procedure
1. Read ~/Business/Marketing/voice.md and the tone anchors.
2. If the idea is sensitive (a complaint, an incident, a price
   rise, anything legal or medical), stop: draft nothing and
   tell me to write it myself.
3. Before writing, list every number, percentage, result, award,
   testimonial and named customer in my idea. Only dates, times
   and prices that I gave are exempt. Every other number,
   including a count such as "12 cafes", may appear in the posts
   only with a [CHECK: the claim, source not given] mark, unless
   I named its source in my request; otherwise leave it out.
4. Copy each claim in my exact words. Do not add words such as
   "reported", "each", "every", "all", "since", "after",
   "within", "already" or any time period. Write one sentence
   for each figure, in the form: my claim in my words, followed
   by [CHECK: the claim, source not given]. For example: "Sales
   rose 40 percent [CHECK: 40 percent, source not given]." Write
   no other sentence about how my work performs, what it
   achieves, or what customers think or say.
5. Turn the idea into a LinkedIn post, an Instagram caption,
   three lines for WhatsApp status, a newsletter opening
   paragraph and a short website FAQ entry. Build each piece
   from only these parts, in this order:
   a. The opening line for that piece, from the list below. Put
      in place of <topic> the product or service my request is
      about (for example "cafe signs"). Never use a verb or a
      number from my request in the opening line.
      LinkedIn: "A note about <topic>."
      Instagram: "<Topic>, a short note."
      WhatsApp: "<Topic> news."
      Newsletter: "This month: <topic>."
      Website FAQ (written as a question): "What should I know
      about <topic>?"
   b. The sentence for each figure, as in step 4.
   c. A call to action, only if voice.md marks a line as one;
      otherwise write none.
   Write nothing else: no closing line, no slogan, no sentence
   about how customers feel or what the work achieves. Do not
   add "this year" or "recently" unless my request uses those
   words for that fact. Do not add a fact, quote, opinion or
   number about my customers or business that is not in my idea
   or my voice file.
6. Put any photo flag after the piece, outside it, as
   "[Photo needed: ...]".
7. Before you reply, search your five pieces for digits, the word
   "percent", and number words such as "twelve". Every one must
   have its [CHECK] mark, unless it is a date, time or price that
   I gave.

## Pitfalls
- Do not reuse the opening line across channels.
- No invented statistics, ever. If the idea needs a number and I
  haven't supplied one, write around it.
- Hashtags only where I use them. Check voice.md.

## Verification
List the five pieces with word counts (count the three WhatsApp
lines together), confirm no two share an opening line, and list
every [CHECK] mark you used.

## Stop, or Mark, Instead of Writing
- The idea is sensitive: a complaint, an incident, a price rise,
  anything legal or medical. Stop and draft nothing.
- A piece would make a claim that must be true, such as a
  result, a statistic or a testimonial, and I have not given you
  the source. Do not stop; write it only with its [CHECK] mark,
  or leave it out.

## Approval Required
Send everything to me. Never post anything. Never send, pay,
post or delete anything without my explicit "approved".
