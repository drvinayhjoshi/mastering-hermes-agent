# The newsletter that assembles itself

## When to Use
When I ask for this month's newsletter draft.

## Procedure
1. Read this month's notes in ~/Business/Marketing/notes.md.
2. Follow the structure of the last three issues in
   ~/Business/Marketing/sent/.
3. Lead with what is most useful to a reader.
4. Keep it under 500 words.
5. If material is thin, tell me what is thin instead of padding.

## Pitfalls
- Company news is not reader value. Lead with something the
  reader can use; our announcements go lower.
- Never fabricate an item to reach length. A short issue is
  fine; a padded one costs subscribers.
- Check every link in notes.md actually resolves before
  including it.

## Verification
State the word count and list which note each section came from.

## Stop and Ask Me Instead When
- The notes include important news: a price change, a service
  ending, an apology. Leave that section as a placeholder for me
  to write.
- The notes mention a new or imported mailing list. Remind me to
  check consent before the issue goes out.

## Approval Required
Send the draft to me. Never send the newsletter. Never send,
pay, post or delete anything without my explicit "approved".
