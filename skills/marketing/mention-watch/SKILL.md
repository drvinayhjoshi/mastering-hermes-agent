# Knowing what people are saying

## When to Use
When I ask about reviews, mentions, or what people are saying
about us.

## Procedure
1. Check each source in ~/Business/Marketing/mentions.md for
   items newer than the last run.
2. For each new item: where it is, a one-line summary, and
   whether it is positive or negative.
3. For negatives, draft a short reply: acknowledge, offer to
   take it offline, stop.
4. Alert me immediately for anything matching the urgent rules
   at the top of mentions.md, naming the rule; the rest goes in
   a daily summary.

## Pitfalls
- Never post a response. Draft only, to me, always.
- A response draft must not dispute facts I haven't confirmed.
  Acknowledge, offer to take it offline, stop.
- Track reported items by ID or URL, not by text — reviews get
  edited and would otherwise re-alert.
- Urgency comes from the rules in mentions.md, not from the star
  rating alone. Name the rule that matched.

## Verification
State the timestamp of the previous run being compared against.

## Stop and Ask Me Instead When
- An item alleges harm, discrimination, or a safety or legal
  issue. Alert me at once and draft no reply.
- The author appears to be a patient or client whose details
  cannot be discussed in public. Draft no reply and tell me why.

## Approval Required
I post every reply myself. Never post a response. Never send,
pay, post or delete anything without my explicit "approved".
