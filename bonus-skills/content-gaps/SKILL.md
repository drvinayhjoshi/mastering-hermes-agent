# Finding what you should have written about

## When to Use
When I ask what we should write about, or for
content gaps.

## Procedure
1. Read ~/Business/Marketing/site-map.md.
2. Review what the named competitors publish.
3. Find subjects they answer that we don't, and that our
   customers would plausibly search for.
4. Give me ten, ranked by relevance to what we sell, each with
   one line on what the piece would need to say.

## Pitfalls
- Do not report search volumes unless a real data source is
  connected. Estimated volumes invented by a model are
  worthless and will mislead planning.
- A subject we cover badly is a gap. Check quality of
  coverage, not just presence of a page.
- Exclude subjects we deliberately avoid; the list is in
  site-map.md under "not for us".

## Verification
For each gap, name the competitor page that prompted it.

## Approval Required
None; the list comes to me.
