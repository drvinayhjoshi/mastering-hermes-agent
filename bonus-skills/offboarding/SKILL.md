# The leaver checklist that closes every door

## When to Use
When I tell you someone is leaving and ask for their
offboarding checklist.

## Procedure
1. Read ~/Business/People/offboarding-master.md and the
   person's role in ~/Business/People/roles.md.
2. Build the checklist, dated from the last working day:
   - accounts to disable (email, software, shared drives,
     messaging, the Hermes profiles and channels they used)
   - shared passwords and API keys to rotate
   - devices, keys, cards and property to recover
   - documents and clients to hand over, and to whom
   - responsibilities to reassign
   - records the business must keep, and for how long
3. Put anything that must happen on the last day, not after,
   at the top.
4. Send the checklist to me, with an owner for each item.

## Pitfalls
- Access is removed on the last day, not "soon after".
- Shared credentials the person knew must be rotated, even if
  their own account is disabled.
- Never delete the person's files or mailbox. Hand-over and
  retention come first; deletion is my decision.
- Keep personal details (reason for leaving, pay, health) out
  of the checklist.

## Verification
Every item in the master list appears as included, not
applicable (with reason), or done.

## Stop and Ask Me Instead When
- The departure is contentious or involves a dispute. Remove
  access at the moment you are told, then talk to me.
- The person held admin rights to your systems or finances.

## Approval Required
You prepare the checklist only. I or the named owner carry
out and confirm each item. Never send, pay, post or delete
anything without my explicit "approved".
