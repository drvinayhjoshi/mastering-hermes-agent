# Bonus skills

Seven skills that sit outside the book's twenty-five playbooks, written in the same format. They matter a great deal to some businesses and not at all to others. Read each one before you install it, adjust the folder paths, and run it by hand before you schedule it.

| Skill | What it does | Risk | Your approval | Tested |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [`subscription-audit`](subscription-audit/SKILL.md) | Finds every recurring charge in twelve months of statements; flags duplicates, price rises and forgotten tools | Low | You decide what to cancel | Tested once |
| [`competitor-watch`](competitor-watch/SKILL.md) | Snapshots competitors' public pricing pages and reports substantive changes only | Low | None; it reports | Tested once, on saved copies of pages |
| [`ad-guardrail`](ad-guardrail/SKILL.md) | Checks daily ad spend against budget pace and cost per conversion; never changes a campaign | Low | None; it reports | Tested once |
| [`content-gaps`](content-gaps/SKILL.md) | Finds questions your customers search for that your site doesn't answer | Low | You choose what to write | Tested three times (version 1.2.0) |
| [`month-end-close`](month-end-close/SKILL.md) | Assembles the month's statements, invoices, bills and receipts; lists exceptions for your bookkeeper | Medium | Your bookkeeper posts every entry | Tested three times (version 1.5.0) |
| [`recruitment-shortlist`](recruitment-shortlist/SKILL.md) | Lays applications side by side against criteria you wrote first; drafts interview questions | High | A person reads every application | Tested once |
| [`offboarding`](offboarding/SKILL.md) | Builds the leaver checklist: accounts, credentials, devices, hand-over, records | Medium | You confirm each item is done | Tested once |

`recruitment-shortlist` needs particular care. In some places, using automated tools to screen job applicants is regulated. Check before you use it, and never let it make a decision about a person.
